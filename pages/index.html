Slides from your Obsidian notes

Write a normal note. Slidey turns it into a styled slide deck you can present with a USB clicker and save as a PDF.

Get started Latest release

Slidey is a plugin for Obsidian. You keep writing in Markdown, the way you already do. Slidey shows the slides live next to your note, lets you pick a layout and a look for each slide, and runs the finished deck full screen. A presentation clicker moves through it.

What it does

Three ways to write

Every heading becomes a slide, or --- splits slides, or only the parts you mark become slides and the rest of the note stays private.

Slide menu

Type / in a slide note to add a new slide, a layout, a background picture, speaker notes and more, without remembering any syntax.

Layouts and styles

8 layouts decide where things go. 5 editable styles decide colors and fonts. Mix any layout with any style. Text that doesn't fit shrinks by itself.

Pictures that fit

Pictures shrink to fit the slide, sit side by side in a row, or fill the background, darkened so text stays readable.

Present with a clicker

Full-screen present mode, speaker notes, and USB presentation clickers that just work, even when the note has focus.

Save as PDF

One command saves the deck as a PDF or a PowerPoint file, one page per slide, backgrounds included. HTML export is there too.

A 10-second example

This note becomes three slides:

---
slides: headings
style: night
---
# My talk

## Why it matters
%% layout=two-column %%
- Point one
- Point two

## Thank you
%% bg=sunset.jpg dim=40% %%

The first heading is the title slide. The second shows its bullets in two columns. The third puts a darkened photo behind the text. Try it yourself.
