Pictures

Pictures from your vault work as they do in any note, and they always fit on the slide.

A picture on a slide

## Our new office
![[office.jpg]]
  • Both ![[office.jpg]] and ![](office.jpg) work.
  • A big or tall picture shrinks to fit under the heading and keeps its shape. It never runs off the slide.
  • For a picture beside text, use the image-left or image-right layout. For a picture that fills the slide with text on top, use image-full.

Backgrounds

Put a picture or a color behind the whole slide with bg= in a marker:

MarkerResult
%% bg=photo.jpg %%Picture fills the slide (cropped to fit)
%% bg=photo.jpg dim=40% %%Same, darkened by 40% so text on top stays readable
%% bg=photo.jpg fit=contain %%Whole picture shown, nothing cropped
%% bg=#224466 %%Solid color
%% bg=[[My photo.png]] %%A vault link works too, also for names with spaces

Pick from a list

Type bg= inside a marker and a list of your vault's pictures opens, with the ones next to your note first. Keep typing to narrow it down, then press Enter. Slidey writes the name so it always finds that exact picture.

A web address works as well: %% bg=https://example.com/photo.jpg %%. Type /bg for the slide menu versions.
