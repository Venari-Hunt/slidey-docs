Presenting

Preview while you write, then present full screen with the keyboard or a USB clicker.

The preview

  • Open it with the slides icon in the left ribbon, or the Show slide preview command. It opens beside your note. Change where it opens in Settings → Slidey → Slide preview mode (split, tab or sidebar).
  • It updates as you type (Auto reload setting) and follows your cursor: click in a slide's text and the preview jumps there.
  • The preview shows the note that was active when you opened it. After switching notes, click the slides icon again (or type /preview in the note) to show the new note's slides. The Show slide preview command opens and closes the preview.

Slide overview

Run Show slide overview, or type /overview in a slide note. A panel opens in the right sidebar with a small picture of every slide. Click one to jump to that slide in your note; the preview follows. The slide your cursor is in gets an orange outline. Drag the panel's top edge to make it taller and see more slides at once.

Present mode

Run Present slides (fullscreen). The deck fills the screen and hides everything else in Obsidian. Move the mouse to show an Exit button in the top-right corner, or press Esc.

Tip: give it a hotkey in Settings → Hotkeys (search “Present slides”).

Two screens: speaker view

With a projector or second screen connected, run Present with speaker view (two screens), type /speaker in a slide note, or press S in the preview.

  • The slides open in their own window, move to the second screen and go full screen by themselves.
  • Your screen shows the speaker view: the current slide, the next slide, your speaker notes, a timer and the time of day.
  • Your clicker works from either window. Both stay on the same slide.
  • Press Esc on the slides, or click End in the speaker view, to finish.

With only one screen, the slides open in a normal window you can drag to the projector yourself.

USB presentation clickers

Most clickers send Page Down / Page Up or the arrow keys, which Slidey understands. You don't have to click the slides first. Slidey passes these keys to the deck even when your note has focus.

If your clicker's “blank screen” or “laser” buttons do something odd, that's because they send other keys (often B or .). B blacks out the screen; press it again to come back.

Speaker notes

## My slide
Text everyone sees.

%% notes %%
Only I see this, in the speaker view.

Everything after %% notes %% on that slide goes to the speaker view, not the slide. In separators mode, a line starting with note: works the same way.
