Layouts & styles

A layout decides where things go on a slide. A style decides the colors and fonts. Any layout works with any style.

## My slide
%% layout=image-left style=paper %%
![[photo.jpg]]
Text beside the picture.

Set them for one slide with a marker (above), or for the whole deck in the note's properties: layout: title, style: night. A slide's marker wins over the deck setting.

Layouts

8 layouts come with Slidey. They never change colors or fonts.

NameWhat it does
titleBig centered title, for the opening slide
sectionSection divider between parts of a talk
two-columnText and bullets flow into two columns
image-leftPicture on the left, text on the right
image-rightText on the left, picture on the right
image-fullPicture fills the slide, text on top
quoteLarge quote
codeRoom for a code block

Styles

A style is a set of colors and fonts: background, text color, accent color (headings and links), heading font, body font, text size and alignment. 5 styles come with Slidey: night, paper, clean, bold and ocean.

Edit them, or add your own, in Settings → Slidey → Slide styles. Each style shows a live preview while you edit, and its dot color matches the dots beside your slides in the editor.

Fonts must be installed on your computer. If a font doesn't show, check its exact name in your system's font list.

One-off changes

To change one slide without making a new style, add any of these to its marker:

%% font="Open Sans" color=#eeeeee accent=red size=1.4 %%
KeyChangesExample
fontFont for headings and textfont="Georgia"
colorText colorcolor=#eeeeee
accentHeading and link coloraccent=orange
sizeText size: a number multiplies it, or give a sizesize=1.4, size=36px

These always win over the slide's style.

Transitions

A transition is how a slide appears when you move to it. Put one under a slide heading:

%% transition=zoom %%

Choices: fade, slide, zoom, convex, concave, none. Use two at once for in and out, like transition="fade-in slide-out". For the whole deck, add transition: fade to the note properties, or pick one in Settings → Slidey.

Text that fits

When a slide has more text than fits, like a long list or a big paragraph, Slidey makes that slide's text smaller until nothing runs off the bottom or the right edge. It never goes below half size, and slides that fit keep their normal size. PDF exports get the same result.

To turn it off, go to Settings → Slidey → Shrink text to fit, or add fitText: false to one deck's properties. If a slide ends up very small, split it into two slides.

Presets (older)

Before layouts and styles were separate, Slidey had presets: a layout and a look in one. They still work (%% preset=quote %% or preset: cover), and you can edit them in Settings → Slidey → Slide presets. The 7 presets are cover, section, quote, image-left, bullets, code and image-bg. For new decks, layouts and styles are more flexible.

Looks by heading level

In headings mode, Settings → Slidey → Heading levels lets each heading level pick a layout, a style and a preset. For example, # gets the title layout and ## gets two-column. A marker under a heading still wins.
